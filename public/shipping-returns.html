Domestic Shipping Policy

At Ethnic Elegance, we understand the excitement of receiving your order. We partner with India's most trusted logistics providers to ensure your garments reach you safely and on time. All orders are processed within 24-48 hours of being placed.

  • check_circle Standard Delivery: 5-7 business days across major metro cities.
  • check_circle Express Delivery: 2-3 business days (Available for select pin codes at an additional charge of ₹250).
  • check_circle Remote Locations: Please allow 7-10 business days for delivery to remote areas.

International Orders

We ship worldwide! International shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on the weight of the package and the destination country.

Duties & Taxes

Please note that import duties, taxes, and brokerage fees are not included in the product price or shipping and handling cost. These charges are the buyer's responsibility as we are only charging the transportation fee for your order. You may check with your country’s customs office to determine if there will be additional costs.

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your purchase. If you are not completely satisfied, we offer a hassle-free return and exchange policy. Items must be returned unworn, unwashed, and with all original tags attached.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)? expand_more
Yes, we offer Cash on Delivery for domestic orders up to ₹20,000. For orders above this amount, we require prepaid payment methods for security reasons.
Can I cancel my order after placing it? expand_more
You can cancel your order within 24 hours of placing it directly from your 'My Orders' page. Once the order has been processed or shipped, it cannot be canceled, but you can initiate a return after delivery.
What happens if I receive a damaged product? expand_more
We are truly sorry about that! Please email us at support@ethnicelegance.com with photos of the damaged item within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange for a free pickup and a replacement or full refund immediately.
How long does the refund process take? expand_more
Once your returned item is received and passes our quality check, the refund is initiated within 48 hours. The amount will reflect in your original payment source within 5-7 business days.
